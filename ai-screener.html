AI Bottleneck Screener

This screener looks for companies positioned at the physical and technological bottlenecks of AI expansion. Instead of screening for companies simply labeled “AI,” it focuses on infrastructure where demand can outgrow supply: power, grid equipment, cooling, memory, networking, optics, advanced packaging and semiconductor manufacturing.

Long-term focus: identify businesses that may benefit from multi-year AI infrastructure spending, then apply financial-quality and valuation checks before treating them as investment candidates.

⚡ Power & Grid

Generation, turbines, electrical distribution, transformers and grid capacity can constrain data-center growth.

❄️ Cooling & Power Management

High-density AI racks require more sophisticated thermal management, UPS and electrical infrastructure.

🧠 HBM & Memory

Accelerators need rapidly expanding high-bandwidth memory capacity and advanced memory packaging.

🌐 Networking & Optics

Scale-out AI clusters increase bandwidth demand across switches, optical links, interconnects and DSPs.

🏭 Foundry, Packaging & Equipment

Leading-edge wafers, advanced packaging and semiconductor tools remain key capacity constraints.
